## Common visual semantics

- Green (`#E7F4F0`): initial scope, core controls, or supported outcomes.
- Gray (`#F0F3F7`): ordinary processing or operational steps.
- Amber (`#FFF0D6`): human review, decisions, or verification.
- Red (`#FBE9E8`): hold, block, rejection, uncertainty, or stale state.
- Violet (`#EEF0FC`): future module capabilities; separately gated.
- A check passing means that the defined check completed with that result. It does not guarantee absence of fraud, establish full compliance, or remove an independent approval requirement.
- Retry/correction loops mean new evidence or a new evaluated version. They do not mean repeatedly running an unchanged failure until it passes.

## Rendering guidance

Use the shared renderer's node/edge typography and title block. Each file declares only direction, node shape/fill, clusters, labels, and relationships. No global background, font size, or DPI is set here. Five diagrams have at most sixteen explicit nodes each. Mermaid source diagrams in the narrative are not modified.
